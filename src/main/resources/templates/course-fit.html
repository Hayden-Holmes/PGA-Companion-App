How Course Fit Works

When sufficient historical data exists at a course, weights are computed by a Python Ridge Regression engine with exponential time decay. Each past player-tournament observation is down-weighted by a 2-year half-life so that recent course editions matter more than old ones. The model learns which SG component (off-the-tee, approach, around-the-green, putting) historically predicts lower finish positions at that specific venue.

🤖 ML · Ridge Regression
Weighted Ridge regression on top-finisher SG data. Exponential decay emphasises recent tournaments. R² reported as model confidence.
📊 Pearson Correlation
Bivariate correlation between each SG stat and finish position, used when Python engine is unavailable.
⚖ Heuristic
Yardage-based tier weights (Long / Balanced / Short) when fewer than 12 historical observations exist.

A history bonus of up to ±1.5 points is always applied for past performance at the exact course.
